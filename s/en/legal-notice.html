Who publishes this site

I Will Not Look Away (iwillnotlookaway.org) is a personal, independent site devoted to documentation, analysis and opinion on international law, institutions and international relations.

The project is run by a single author, Neri Polvani, who publishes under the pseudonym N3R1-70. The author is not a professional journalist.

The site is not registered as a news publication under Italian Law no. 47 of 8 February 1948. It has no fixed publication schedule, carries no advertising and has no commercial purpose. This description concerns the nature and organisation of the project and is not intended to exclude any liability provided for by law.

The content is an exercise of the freedom of expression, reporting, research and criticism protected by Article 21 of the Italian Constitution and Article 10 of the European Convention on Human Rights.

Where it is published

The site is edited and published from Italy. The activity is carried out in accordance with Italian law and European Union law.

How we work

  • Facts are accompanied, where available, by identifiable sources and the relevant dates.
  • Documented facts are kept separate from the author's interpretations, assessments and opinions.
  • Accusations, allegations and hypotheses are not presented as established facts.
  • When sources diverge or contradict each other significantly, this is pointed out.
  • The same standard of assessment is applied regardless of the State, organisation or person involved.
  • People holding public office are covered in relation to their institutional activity and to facts of public interest, with respect for the rights of the person and for the principle that information must be essential to the public interest. People's private lives are not the subject of the site.

Presumption of innocence

Mentioning a person in connection with an investigation, an accusation, an arrest warrant, a sanction or other proceedings does not mean they are guilty. The site distinguishes between the following stages and legal statuses:

  • Preliminary investigation: the person is "under investigation"; no formal charge has yet been brought.
  • Prosecution: the person becomes a "defendant".
  • Precautionary measures (pre-trial detention, house arrest, etc.): restrictions of personal liberty imposed before a final judgment, which do not establish guilt.
  • Arrest warrant (national or international): an act issued by a judicial authority, whose enforcement and validity depend on the legal context. "Fugitive" means a person subject to an arrest warrant who has not been arrested or surrendered.
  • Individual restrictive measures (sanctions decided by the European Union, the UN Security Council or individual States, such as asset freezes or entry bans): foreign-policy acts that do not establish criminal liability.
  • Administrative, disciplinary or criminal penalties: only the latter, once final, presuppose a finding of criminal liability.
  • Final judgment: only after an irrevocable conviction can a person be called "guilty", in line with Article 27(2) of the Italian Constitution. No one is presented as criminally guilty without a final conviction.

The site documents acts and statements of third-party authorities and bodies without taking the place of the competent bodies or issuing judicial verdicts.

Lists of people

Sections that list people are based on public and verifiable inclusion criteria, set out at the top of each page. The title of each section matches the legal status of the people it contains: "Fugitives" only for people subject to an arrest warrant.

Each entry shows, where available: the issuing authority, the nature of the measure, the date, the link to the official source, any position or denial by the person concerned, and the date of the last check.

The lists are re-checked against official sources at least every six months. Revocations, dismissals, acquittals and final judgments are reflected at the next check, and immediately when reported with a document.

A person is removed from a list when the measure is revoked, the investigation is closed or they are acquitted. For people who hold or have held public office, the removal may remain on record, with the outcome clearly shown.

The presence of a name on a list does not in itself amount to a finding of guilt.

Open questions

Content labelled "Open question" raises questions about facts or circumstances that have not yet been clarified. It does not attribute criminal or civil liability.

This content separates documented elements from missing information, also reports the elements that weaken each hypothesis and, where possible, names the institutions or bodies able to provide clarification. Official answers and clarifications are taken into account when updating the content, even when they refute the hypotheses.

Opinions, manifestos and petitions

The "Opinions" and "Manifestos" sections express the author's point of view and are not official statements of any institution or organisation mentioned.

The site distinguishes between two kinds of petitions:

  • petitions started by the author, labelled as such, for which the author is responsible like any other content of his;
  • petitions and appeals started by others, labelled as external initiatives and attributed to their organisers. Linking to them does not imply endorsing all their content.

Links reported as unlawful are checked and, if the report is well founded, removed. Reports should be sent to editor@iwillnotlookaway.org.

Corrections and replies

Anyone who believes that content is inaccurate, incomplete or harmful to their rights can send a written report to corrections@iwillnotlookaway.org.

The report should indicate the page concerned, the passage disputed, the reasons and, where possible, supporting sources or documents.

Reports receive a reply within 30 days. When they are well founded, the content is corrected, supplemented or removed, with a dated and visible update or correction note where appropriate.

The author may consider publishing replies and clarifications sent by the people or organisations concerned, with due regard for relevance, fairness and the rights of those involved.

Anonymous reports may be considered when they contain enough elements to check the facts disputed. Where there are reasonable doubts about the identity of the sender, further verification may be requested.

Messages containing threats, insults, unlawful material or irrelevant personal information are not published. Public correction notes do not show the sender's email address or other personal data, unless publishing them is lawful and necessary.

Translations

The site's content is available in eleven languages, to make the information accessible and widely available internationally. The Italian version is the editorial reference for checking and interpreting the other language versions.

Translations may differ in wording or syntax because of the features of each language. In the event of substantial differences, the Italian text prevails and the versions concerned are corrected.

Translation errors can be reported to corrections@iwillnotlookaway.org.

Sources, quotations, images and external links

The site uses institutional sources, public documents, publications and material from third-party organisations and people. Sources are given, where possible, through direct links, document references and publication dates.

Quotations, summaries and excerpts are used for the purposes of criticism and discussion, within the limits of Article 70 of Italian Law no. 633 of 22 April 1941, with the author and source indicated.

The texts of official acts of States and public administrations are reproduced under Article 5 of the same law. Documents of international and non-governmental organisations are quoted within the limits of their respective terms of use.

The site's illustrations and infographics are made by the author with the help of artificial-intelligence tools, based on data from the sources cited. They are not photographs of real events.

Anyone who believes that content infringes their copyright can write to editor@iwillnotlookaway.org: the content is checked and, if the report is well founded, removed or corrected.

Citing a source does not necessarily mean sharing its positions. The site is not responsible for the content of third-party sites, which may change or disappear over time.

Personal data

The data controller is Neri Polvani. For requests about personal data, write to privacy@iwillnotlookaway.org.

Emails received (reports, corrections, contact requests) are used only to reply and to handle the request. We process them on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying and in checking the accuracy of the content (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). When a request concerns your rights over personal data, we handle it to comply with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) and Articles 15–22 GDPR). Emails are not passed on to anyone.

The @iwillnotlookaway.org addresses are forwarded, through Namecheap's forwarding service, to an inbox managed by Google.

We keep emails for 12 months after the matter is closed. When a report claims harm, we keep it for up to 5 years, the period during which a claim for damages can be brought, and in any case until any dispute is concluded.

For analytics tools, cookies and your rights, see the Privacy and cookies page.
