Privacy and cookies

What data this site collects, which tools it uses and how you can choose.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short. If you open the site and do nothing, or if you decline, we only use technical tools and an anonymous visit counter, with no cookies. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel are switched on only if you click “Accept”. You can change your mind at any time from “Manage cookies”, at the bottom of every page.

Who handles the data

The data controller is Neri Polvani, author of the site, who also writes under the pseudonym N3R1-70. For any question about your data, write to iwillnotlookaway@gmail.com.

The site is run by one person and has no data protection officer (one is not required for a site like this).

What happens when you open a page

The server that hosts the site. The site is published on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub (part of Microsoft). Like any server, it receives the IP address of each visitor. GitHub logs it for security reasons, under its own privacy statement. We have no access to these logs.

Umami, the visit counter. We count visits with Umami. It uses no cookies, does not recognise you from one visit to the next and does not follow you to other sites. It uses the IP address only to work out the approximate country and city, then discards it without saving it. We only see overall figures: pages read, where visits come from, type of device. The data is kept on Umami Cloud servers in Frankfurt, Germany: it does not leave the European Union. We use it on the basis of a legitimate interest: knowing which pieces are read (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Fonts. The site's fonts are stored on our own server. Reading a page downloads nothing from Google Fonts.

Your cookie choice. To remember whether you accepted or declined, we store a small piece of technical information in your browser, called iwnla_consent, together with the date of your choice. It is not a cookie and it does not leave your device. It lasts 180 days: after that, the banner comes back and we ask you again. It is a technical tool, so it does not require consent (Article 122 of the Italian Data Protection Code).

Only if you click “Accept”

With your consent we switch on two tools from other companies. Before the click they are not loaded: your browser contacts neither Google nor Meta.

Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC). It measures traffic: how many people read, where they come from, how long they stay. We have switched off advertising features and “Google signals”: the data is not used to show you ads. According to Google, for visitors from the European Union the IP address is used only to work out the location and is then discarded. Google Analytics keeps visit data for 2 months and data tied to an individual user for 14 months, then deletes it. Google's privacy policy.

Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). It measures the results of the campaigns the site pays for on Facebook and Instagram: for example, how many people arrive from a sponsored post. If you have a Facebook or Instagram account, Meta may link the visit to your profile, under its own privacy policy. For collecting this data and sending it to Meta we are joint controllers with Meta (Court of Justice of the EU, case C-40/17, Fashion ID, 29 July 2019). What Meta does afterwards is up to Meta.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 122 of the Italian Data Protection Code). You can withdraw it whenever you like, with no consequences: the whole site stays readable.

Data in the United States. Google and Meta may transfer data to the United States. Both companies take part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognised as adequate in Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023. An appeal against this decision is pending before the Court of Justice of the EU (case C-703/25 P). If the decision is annulled, we will update this page.

How to change your choice

At the bottom of every page you will find “Manage cookies”. The banner reappears and you can choose again. If you switch from “Accept” to “Decline”, we immediately delete the _ga, _ga_…, _fbp and _fbc cookies and reload the page, so Google and Meta stop working.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Email

If you write to us, your message reaches a Gmail inbox (Google). We use your address only to reply or to handle a correction request. We do not pass it on to anyone. The site has no newsletter and does not collect email addresses.

Links to other sites

The site links to outside sources, to Facebook, to LinkedIn and to petitions on change.org. These are plain links: none of these services is loaded in our pages. If you open them, those sites' rules apply.

The “Share” button uses your device's sharing function, or copies the link. It does not go through outside services.

What we do not do

We do not sell data. We do not run ads. We do not build reader profiles. We do not make automated decisions about people.

Pages in Chinese and Russian

The pages in Chinese and Russian use only Umami. They do not load Google Analytics or Meta Pixel, so they do not show the banner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data about you, to correct it or delete it, to restrict its use, to receive it in a readable format, and you can object to its use (Articles 15–22 GDPR). Write to iwillnotlookaway@gmail.com: we reply within one month.

If you think your data is being handled improperly, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of the country where you live (Article 77 GDPR). For Google's and Meta's tools you can also contact them directly.

Legal references

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR); Italian Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003), Article 122; Guidelines of the Italian Data Protection Authority on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021.

Changes to this page

If we change the tools we use, we update this page and the date at the top. If the change requires new consent, the banner will ask you again.
